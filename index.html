Editorial correspondence
THE MERIDIANARCHIVES / UNSIGNED

Found these in the old local-news box. I can remember some of the stories, at least.

Jane wouldn't let us crop the photograph of the students holding their debate trophy. She'd spent all afternoon with their coach.

I'm fairly sure the pesticide story went to press while we were still in our old office. We'd moved out by the beginning of 1992.

Harriet had left before the debate team story came in. She would have liked that one, I think.

Elliot was the one who went to see the restored reading hall. He came back talking about the smell of fresh paint.

The library feature ran two years before the new millennium. Hard to believe that was so long ago.

We pulled out the pesticide cutting again when we were preparing the school story; it was already an old clipping by then.

Hope that helps sort the records out.
